A PRACTICAL CAMPUS COMPANION

Find your way.
Make it yours.

Use this guide as a starter directory for student life. Confirm actual locations, schedules and policies with official college sources before relying on them.

01 / EVERYDAY LIFE

Campus essentials

  • Check your class timetable and notice board regularly.
  • Keep your student identity card and required materials ready.
  • Know where to find the library, office and student support services.
  • Use bins, keep shared spaces clean and respect quiet study areas.

02 / GET INVOLVED

Find your community

Societies and student groups can be a place to practise collaboration, leadership, performance, writing, photography and production.

  1. Explore available groups.
  2. Attend an introductory meeting.
  3. Ask about schedules and joining requirements.
  4. Contribute responsibly and credit collaborators.

04 / RESPONSIBLE MEDIA

Tell stories ethically

  • Ask permission before photographing or recording people.
  • Verify information before publishing a campus story.
  • Distinguish facts, opinions and sponsored content.
  • Protect personal information and respect copyright.
